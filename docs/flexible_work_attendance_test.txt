# Flexible Work and Office Attendance Policy

**Policy ID:** TEST-HR-2026-07

**Effective Date:** October 24, 2026

## 1. Remote Work Eligibility
Employees may work remotely for up to four days per week, provided they receive written approval from their department manager.

## 2. Mandatory Office Attendance
All employees must attend the office every Tuesday and Thursday. These attendance days are mandatory even for employees approved for remote work.

## 3. Availability During Working Hours
Employees working remotely must be available on the company messaging platform and respond to work-related messages within 30 minutes during normal working hours.

## 4. Schedule Change Requests
Employees must submit requests to change their regular remote-work schedule at least three business days in advance. The department manager must approve the change in writing.

## 5. Equipment and Security
Employees must use company-approved devices to access internal HR systems. Employees must not share their login credentials with other people.
